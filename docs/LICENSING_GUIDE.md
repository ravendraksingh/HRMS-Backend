# Licensing Guide for HRMS Backend

## Overview

This guide provides recommendations for licensing the HRMS Backend application for commercial sale by Niyava Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## Recommended License Type

### **Proprietary/Commercial License** ✅

Since you intend to **sell** this software to customers, you should use a **proprietary/commercial license**. This is the correct choice because:

- ✅ Allows you to retain full ownership and control
- ✅ Prevents unauthorized copying and distribution
- ✅ Enables you to charge for licenses
- ✅ Protects your intellectual property
- ✅ Allows you to set usage restrictions

### ❌ NOT Recommended: Open Source Licenses

Do **NOT** use open source licenses (MIT, GPL, Apache, etc.) if you want to sell the software, as they typically allow free use, modification, and redistribution.

## License Components

### 1. **Copyright Notice**

- Clearly state copyright ownership: "Copyright (c) 2024 Niyava Technologies Pvt. Ltd."
- Include "All rights reserved"

### 2. **License Grant**

- Specify what the customer is allowed to do
- Define scope of use (internal use, number of users, etc.)
- Set geographic or deployment restrictions if needed

### 3. **Restrictions**

- No copying, modification, or redistribution
- No reverse engineering
- No use for competitive purposes
- No sublicensing

### 4. **Term and Termination**

- Define license duration (perpetual, annual, monthly)
- Specify termination conditions
- Define what happens upon termination

## Licensing Models for Commercial Sale

### Option 1: **Per-Organization License** (Recommended for B2B)

**Best for:** Medium to large organizations

**Pricing Model:**

- One-time purchase or annual subscription
- Single organization deployment
- Unlimited users within organization
- Includes updates for a specified period

**Example Pricing:**

- Starter: $5,000 - $10,000 (up to 100 employees)
- Professional: $15,000 - $30,000 (up to 500 employees)
- Enterprise: $50,000+ (unlimited employees)

### Option 2: **Per-User License**

**Best for:** Flexible, scalable pricing

**Pricing Model:**

- Based on number of active users
- Monthly or annual billing
- Tiered pricing (volume discounts)

**Example Pricing:**

- $10-20 per user per month
- $100-200 per user per year
- Discounts for 100+ users

### Option 3: **SaaS Subscription** (Recommended for Cloud)

**Best for:** Cloud-hosted solutions

**Pricing Model:**

- Monthly/annual subscription
- Includes hosting, maintenance, updates
- Tiered plans based on features/users

**Example Pricing:**

- Basic: $500-1,000/month (up to 50 users)
- Professional: $2,000-5,000/month (up to 200 users)
- Enterprise: Custom pricing

### Option 4: **Enterprise License**

**Best for:** Large organizations with custom needs

**Pricing Model:**

- Custom pricing based on requirements
- Includes dedicated support
- Custom development options
- On-premise or cloud deployment

## What to Include in Your License Agreement

### Essential Clauses:

1. **Grant of License**

   - What the customer can do with the software
   - Scope of use (internal, number of users, etc.)

2. **Restrictions**

   - What the customer cannot do
   - Prohibition on copying, modifying, redistributing

3. **Ownership**

   - Clear statement that you own the software
   - Intellectual property rights

4. **Payment Terms**

   - License fees
   - Payment schedule
   - Renewal terms

5. **Support and Maintenance**

   - What's included
   - Response times
   - Update policy

6. **Warranty and Liability**

   - Disclaimer of warranties
   - Limitation of liability
   - Indemnification

7. **Termination**

   - Conditions for termination
   - What happens upon termination

8. **Confidentiality**
   - Protection of proprietary information
   - Non-disclosure requirements

## Legal Recommendations

### ⚠️ Important Legal Considerations:

1. **Consult a Lawyer**

   - Have a legal professional review your license agreement
   - Ensure compliance with local laws
   - Protect your interests properly

2. **Jurisdiction**

   - Specify which country's laws govern the agreement
   - Consider international sales implications

3. **Data Protection**

   - Include clauses about data handling (GDPR, etc.)
   - Specify data ownership and privacy

4. **Updates and Upgrades**

   - Define what's included in maintenance
   - Specify upgrade policies

5. **Audit Rights**
   - Right to verify compliance
   - License usage verification

## License Enforcement Options

### Option 1: **Honor System**

- Trust-based licensing
- Simple to implement
- Lower cost
- ⚠️ Less secure

### Option 2: **License Keys**

- Unique keys per customer
- Validation on startup
- More secure
- Requires implementation

### Option 3: **Online Activation**

- Server-side validation
- Most secure
- Requires infrastructure
- Can track usage
